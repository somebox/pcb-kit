and spotlight (rgb-spotlight) repos were byte-identical; the kit version is the merge of the variants.

  grid of spots where a via of a given size clears other nets on every layer and every mask opening, keeps hole
  spacing and lands on its own net's copper on both layers it joins; then placement for the most slack and a board
  copy per option. A `pcbkit via-spots` (a part, its nets, via sizes) would make it reusable.

  when next touched. The sign switched on 2026-10-18 and keeps only its LED-strip fan-in.

  a shape (placement tables, `build()` calls) that a `pcbkit.board` module could carry: the collision-checked spiral
  placement and zone/stackup setup in dodeca's `pcb_build.py`.

  `set_outline.py`; sign `resilk.py`, `renumber_controller.py`, `build_matrix.py`. siebenuhr's `scripts/layout/score.py`, spotlight's
  `ratsnest.py` and Orbitchron's `drv_place_score.py` and `route_extras.py` are covered by `pcbkit.placement`, `pcbkit.route` and
  `pcbkit cleanup --dangling` (2026-10-14); switch them when next touched (siebenuhr's quick maze route per variant has no kit
  counterpart yet).
